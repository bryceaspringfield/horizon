ProjectionLab's Archived public roadmap

Welcome to the ProjectionLab Changemap archive.

Over the years, you’ve used this board to share ideas, vote on features and help shape ProjectionLab. Thank you to everyone who contributed. Everything you shared here has been saved, and it will continue to inform how we develop ProjectionLab.

With ProjectionLab 5.0, feedback has moved into the app. Open Feedback at the bottom of the left menu to send ideas straight to our team. You can read more in Meet the new ProjectionLab.

This board is now a read-only archive, so it no longer accepts new suggestions, votes or comments. Item statuses show where things stood when the board was archived and aren’t commitments or timelines. To talk through ideas with the community, join the conversation on Discord - #feature-ideas channel.
